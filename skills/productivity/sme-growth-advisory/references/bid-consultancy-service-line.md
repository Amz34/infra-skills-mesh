# Selling the bid-support stack as a paid consultancy (India tenders)

Trigger: owner asks *"can we provide this high-level consultation to firms for bidding?"*,
*"how much can we charge"*, *"how to find the leads"*. Usually arrives right after a big
bid pack has been built — the pack IS the product proof.

Worked case: HMW Engineering Solutions, 2026-09, after the INCOIS ₹1 Cr HVAC CAMC bid pack.
Generator for the delivered PDF: `/home/ubuntu/hmw_bid/mk_consult_offering.py`
(output `HMW_Bid_Consultancy_Feasibility_Pricing_Leads.pdf`, 5 pages, chat tables + PDF).

## 1. Answer shape (three questions, three tables — plus a verdict)

The owner asks exactly three things; answer all three, in this order, tabulated:

1. **Feasibility** — *"can we sell this?"* → what is sellable (service × Ready? × market price),
   then a verdict table: product ready? · demand? · what's missing? · our cost per bid · margin ·
   main risk · VERDICT.
2. **Pricing** — *"how much can we charge?"* → tier table + a market-benchmark table (what rivals
   actually charge, and why we skip the cheap tiers) + cost/margin table + Year-1 ramp table.
3. **Leads** — *"what is the process to find the leads?"* → channel table (effort → expected leads),
   the funnel, and a 90-day plan.

Close with guardrails (ethics/conflict), the asset list (why we can do this today), and
**1–3 concrete questions** (which segment first, which 2 firms for free pilots, whose name on the
invoice). Keep all of it in the chat as fenced code-block tables **and** ship the English PDF.

## 2. What is sellable (the asset → service map)

| Service | Ready? | India market price found |

| Go / No-Go fit report (eligibility + margin reality check) | Yes | ₹5,000–15,000 |

Verdict lines that held up: product ready (proven on a live ₹1 Cr tender) · demand (11 lakh+ MSEs on
GeM, ~68% of orders by count) · missing = 2 case studies, GeM hands-on, **NDA + one-client-per-tender
rule** · our cost ₹2,000–10,000 tools/AI + 6–10 hrs senior review per bid · margin 85–90% at a
₹50,000 pack · main risk = conflict of interest and tender-season capacity.

| 2 — Bid-Ready Pack (per tender) | ₹40,000–75,000 | ₹50 L – ₹2 Cr tenders |
| 3 — Complete Bid Management (per tender) | ₹90,000–1,50,000 | ₹1 Cr – ₹5 Cr tenders |
| 4 — Annual Retainer | ₹3,00,000/yr | 8+ bids/yr |
| Add-ons (site visit, corrigendum note, ROI model, mobilisation plan) | ₹8,000–30,000 each | any tier |
| Success fee — **private firms only** | 0.5–1.5% of contract value | low-upfront clients |

Market scan (2026, quoted by Indian tender/GeM consultants — use as the anchor, never as gospel):

| Market service | Rate found | Our call |
|---|---|---|
| GeM upload only (clerical) | ₹1,500–5,000/bid | **skip** — no margin |
| GeM account mgmt + alerts | ₹24,000–25,000/yr | = Tier 1 |
| Block packages (≈20 bids / 3 months) | ≈₹24,000 | **skip** — volume game |
| Monthly consultant desk | ₹5,000–75,000/mo | Tier 4 = ₹25,000/mo |
| End-to-end bid management | 1.5%–3% of project cost | Tier 3 = only 0.9–1.5% |
| Hybrid: upfront + success fee | ₹10k–50k + 0.25–2% | ours = 0.5–1.5% |

Cost & margin (cost = tools/AI + senior review hours, no new investment):

| Tier | Price | Cost | Margin | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 alerts | ₹15,000 | ₹1,000 | 93% | 3 hrs/yr |
| Tier 2 bid pack | ₹50,000 | ₹5,000 | 90% | 8–10 hrs |

Year-1 ramp that reads as believable (say it out loud — don't promise more):
months 1–2 = list + case studies + **2 free pilot Go/No-Go reports** (₹0) →
months 3–4 = first paid packs + free weekly broadcast (₹40k–1.5 L billed) →
months 5–6 = first retainer conversation + association workshop (₹1.5–3 L) →
months 7–12 = 2–3 retainers + referral partners (₹4–9 L). 3 retainers = ₹9 L revenue,
₹1.2 L cost, ₹7.8 L margin.

## 4. Lead generation — channels, funnel, 90 days

| # | Channel | Time | Expected leads |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bid-result reverse outreach** (GeM/CPPP award + result pages list who bid and lost) | 30 min/day | 10–15 firms/week |
| 2 | Free weekly "tender of the week" broadcast (WhatsApp/Telegram) — the digest is the hook | 1 hr/week | 100–300 members in 2 months |
| 3 | Contractor WhatsApp groups (trade + state) | 20 min/day | 3–5 enquiries/month |
| 4 | OEM / channel partners (chiller, PAC, VFD service firms) | 10 calls | 2–3 tie-ups/month |
| 5 | Trade & MSME associations (FTCCI, MSME-DFO, HVAC bodies) — free 45-min workshop | 1 session | 3–5 leads/session |
| 6 | Referral partners: CA firms (Udyam/GeM work), DSC vendors, BG/insurance brokers (10–15% referral fee) | 5 meetings | 2–4 leads/month |
| 7 | Direct outreach to past participants of the same tenders | 20 msgs | 1–2 replies/week |

Channel 1 is the workhorse: firms that **already bid and lost** have the intent and the documents —
their gap is pricing method and compliance drafting, not awareness. Never pitch to non-bidders.

Funnel (same six steps for every lead): awareness (free alert/session) → first contact (one line:
your item + one live tender + why you will lose; **no brochure**) → 5-question qualification
(trade? turnover + Udyam? how many bids, how many wins? which tender now? who makes your documents?)
→ **free Go/No-Go report on one tender** as the sample → paid Tier 2/3 with a written scope and 50%
advance → after a win, ask for a retainer + 2 referrals.

90-day plan: weeks 1–2 = 100-firm list + offer sheet + 2 case studies · weeks 3–4 = weekly broadcast
+ 50 first messages + NDA/fee note · weeks 5–8 = 2 free Go/No-Go → present the paid pack (₹40k–75k
billed) · weeks 9–12 = testimonials, 2 referral partners, retainer proposal.

## 5. Guardrails (state these unprompted — they are the credibility answer)

- **One tender = one client.** If the family firm is bidding that tender, it cannot advise a
  competitor: disclose first, then choose.
- **Success fee only from private firms**, in a written agreement with a GST invoice. Never any
  payment, gift or favour to a government official — that is touting and it ends the business.
- **Confidentiality:** one client's BOQ / rates / inventory is never reused for another (NDA both
  ways).
- **No fabricated experience, turnover or OEM letters** — the client gets debarred, and the
  consultant carries the liability.
- **Never promise a win.** Promise a compliant, correctly priced, submission-ready bid plus an
  honest Go/No-Go.
- Disclose AI-assisted drafting; the senior review and the accountability are ours.

## 6. Pitfalls

- **Don't price like an EPC consultant** (1.5–3% of project cost) on AMC/MSME work — a ₹1 Cr AMC
  does not carry that fee. Fixed fees that land at 0.9–1.5% equivalent win the deal.
- **Don't compete with the ₹1,500-per-upload commodity market** — undercutting is unwinnable;
  sell the Go/No-Go + pricing method, which nobody cheap can do.
- **Capacity honesty:** bid support is peaky (deadline clusters). Say so in the offer, cap active
  clients, and never take work you cannot submit 2 working days early.
- **Don't re-use client data across bids**, and don't put a client's inventory annexure in the
  marketing case study — anonymise (sector + size + outcome only).
- The free sample must be *one tender's Go/No-Go*, not the whole bid pack — value-not-free.
