# "Why us when AI is cheaper?" — AI-proof positioning + the pain-point lead roster

Two linked deliverables that follow every big bid pack:

1. **The positioning answer** to *"this data any paid AI (Claude / Fable / Astra) can produce,
   why should the client prefer us?"* — asked by the buyer, by the owner, or by us about ourselves.
2. **The lead roster** — PAN-India firms that need the service, each with contact details
   **and a pain point**, ranked by trade fit.

## Part 1 — The positioning answer (6 parts, in this order)

Never defend the documents. Concede the cheap layer fast, then move the money.

### 1. Concede the commoditised layer
The *document* layer is now ~₹2,000/month (Claude/ChatGPT class in India) or an India
tender-AI SaaS licence (AI bid prep + compliance check + "price intelligence" + OCR in ~20
Indian languages; one such service had 1,500+ MSMEs registered). So "bid likhne wale"
consultants lose. Say it out loud — it buys credibility for everything after.

### 2. Capability matrix — what AI does vs what it cannot

| AI DOES (so don't sell it) | AI CANNOT (this is the money) |
|---|---|
| Read/OCR the tender PDF, make a submission checklist | **Real no-loss price** — needs live AMC/BOQ rate data + OEM quotes |
| Technical bid text, profile, ISO/EHS write-ups | **Accountability** — name, GST invoice, NDA, liability when wrong |
| Compliance matrix draft | **Site truth** — chiller condition, as-found survey, actual load |
| BOQ/Excel structure | **Portal execution** — DSC, catalogue, upload on GeM/CPPP |
| Corrigendum summary | **Bank/CA/OEM chain** — 38-month ePBG in the bank's format, genuine OEM letter |
| "Suggested rate range" (historical average = fiction) | **Buyer-side intelligence** — who got disqualified, and why |
| DOCX/PDF export | Saying **"bid MAT karo, loss hoga"** (it will draft a bid for every tender) |
| | Negotiation/representation after L-1 · owning the deadline (3-day assembly, submission eve) |

### 3. Money logic — risk in rupees, fee as insurance

| Risk before/after award | Client's loss | Our fee |
|---|---|---|
| Bid rejected on a document / EMD / DSC error | ₹1 cr AMC gone + ₹20–30 L revenue + 2-year wait | ₹40–50 k advisory = 0.5% insurance |
| Price quoted ~8% below cost | ₹8 L lost over a 3-year AMC | same fee — the rate build-up prevents it |
| ePBG wording rejected by the bank | ₹15 L stuck for weeks | ₹25–40 k to fix the paperwork |
| Fake/borrowed experience certificate | debarment — business shut | never done by us |

### 4. Honest market comparison

| Option | Cost | What the client gets | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude/ChatGPT class | ~₹2,000/mo | text/draft, zero accountability, no real rate, no submit | client does all the work |
| Tender-AI SaaS licence | monthly licence | AI prep + compliance + price *trend* (average), nobody on site, no liability | still no site, no submit, no name on paper |
| Traditional consultant | 1.5–3% or ₹25 k–1.5 L per bid | docs + portal, slow, generic, expensive | capacity-limited, thin rate data |
| **Us** | tier-based below | AI speed **+** real rate **+** site **+** portal **+** name on paper | capacity-limited, so lower volume |

### 5. Tier table with an AI-proof rating

| Tier | Price | Client pain point it kills | AI-proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Document pack (writing only) | ₹15,000–25,000 | "team ke paas time/format nahi" | **LOW** — AI is cheaper; entry/lead magnet only |
| 2 Bid Room (end-to-end) | ₹60,000–1,50,000, or 1–1.5% of value | "reject ho jayega, rate galat lagega, submit kaun karega" | **HIGH** — real rate + site + portal |
| 3 AMC lifecycle retainer | ₹3,00,000–6,00,000 / yr (₹25–50 k/mo) | "contract mil gaya par paisa atak gaya / penalty lag gayi" | **HIGHEST** — "AI kar hi nahi sakta" |
| 4 Price-intel + Go/No-Go watch | ₹15,000–25,000 / month | "har tender pe bid karte hain, 90% waste" | MED-HIGH — sticky, low delivery cost |

Tier 3 scope: post-award mobilisation, milestone billing follow-up, PBG/insurance compliance,
escalation, extension, price-escalation and renewal handling. Charged monthly/quarterly.

### 6. Sales script + the honest limit

> *"Aap bid AI se likhwa sakte hain. Lekin AI aapke chiller ki condition dekhne site pe nahi
> aayega, OEM se genuine authorization letter nahi layega, PBG bank ke format me nahi karayega,
> GeM pe submit nahi karega — aur galat hone pe jawab nahi dega. Hum wo 4 kaam karte hain.
> Likhne ka kaam hum bhi AI se hi karte hain, isliye aapko sasta padta hai."*

- **"AI hamara dushman nahi, mera staff hai"** — using AI internally is *why* we undercut the
  traditional ₹1.5 L consultant at ₹60 k with 85%+ margin.
- **Best customer:** someone who already tried Claude, got rejected twice, and now wants a human.
- **Honest limit (state it):** where the client only needs a format or a letter, we lose to a
  ₹2,000 subscription — leave those. Target ₹50 lakh–5 crore deals where one mistake costs lakhs.

## Part 2 — The pain-point lead roster

### Who is a prospect (AI-era segmentation)

| Segment | Evidence you can see from outside | Pain point (write it in the prospect's words) |
|---|---|---|
| Firm hiring a Tender/GeM/Bid executive | live job post | "No in-house bid capacity; deadlines slip while we hire" |
| Firm hiring "Technical Project Manager cum Tender Manager" | job title | "One person handles bids + delivery; overstretched" |
| HVAC/MEP/fire/facility service firm | trade + GeM/PPP activity | "Price build-up from zero every time; no live AMC/BOQ rate data" |
| Firm with a GeM catalogue but few wins | catalogue/portal presence | "Compliance and catalogue upkeep gap; docs rejected" |
| Firm that lost / was disqualified, AMC expiring | award-result pages, tender results | "We lost on price or paperwork and don't know which" |

### Roster deliverable spec (what shipped and worked)

One English XLSX, 6 sheets:

| Sheet | Content |
|---|---|
| `Leads PAN-India` | Firm · city · sector · **pain point** · evidence link · email · phone · website · LinkedIn · priority |
| `Tiers with Pain Points` | the 4 tiers: scope, deliverables, timeline, price, client pain, AI-proof verdict |
| `AI vs HMW` | the capability matrix (AI / tender-AI SaaS / traditional consultant / us) |
| `Money Logic (Why They Pay)` | risk → rupees → fee (insurance framing) |
| `Method and Honesty` | sources, selection method, priority meaning, verification flags, limits |
| `First 90 Days` | week-by-week: shortlist → 10 emails + 10 LinkedIn messages → 2 free pilots |

**Priority ladder:** A+ = exact trade fit *and* live demand (firm advertising our exact role) ·
A = adjacent trade (HVAC/MEP/fire/facility/electrical) · B = partial fit · C = low fit (IT,
education) — keep C as backup volume, don't lead with it.

**Pain points must be evidence-derived**, never generic marketing copy: read the job post /
tender result and translate it ("khud *Tender & Estimation Executive – HVAC* hire kar rahe hain
= in-house capacity nahi hai").

### Honesty + compliance guardrails (non-negotiable, put them in the file)

- Contacts come from public sources; carry a per-row flag `[verified]` / `[check-domain]` /
  `not found`. Never invent a website, email or LinkedIn URL — write "no company page found".
- Tell the owner to **confirm one contact before any bulk outreach** (brand risk + DPDP-style
  consent care). No bulk blast of unverified addresses.
- Never guarantee a win. Promise: *compliant, correctly priced, submission-ready bid + an honest
  Go/No-Go*. Experience certificates, turnover and OEM letters must be real or the client is
  debarred. No payment to any official. One tender → one client (conflict rule).
- Timing: run outreach **after** our own live bid closes/submits, because that pack is case
  study #1 ("hamara apna ₹1 cr ka live bid pack") — the strongest proof asset in the pitch.
