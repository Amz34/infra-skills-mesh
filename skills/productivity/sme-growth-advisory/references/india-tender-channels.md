# India — where live work is published (service/AMC trades)

Adaptable to any local service trade: swap the keywords, keep the portals.

## Portals (link these in the tracker sheet, not just in chat)
| Portal | What it carries |
|---|---|
| `gem.gov.in` | Most central AMC / repair / "Custom Bid for Services" work |
| `eprocure.gov.in` | CPPP — central ministries, PSUs |
| `etenders.gov.in` | Central e-tenders |
| `tender.telangana.gov.in` | Telangana state tenders |
| `ireps.gov.in` | Indian Railways |
| `defproc.gov.in` | Defence |
| PSU sites | NTPC, BHEL, AAI, metro, cantonment/railway hospitals |
| Aggregators (free tier) | tenderkart.in, tender18.com, classictenders.com |

## Keyword recipes (search these on each portal)
`AMC air conditioner` · `chiller AMC` · `HVAC maintenance` · `cleanroom HVAC` ·
`VRV AMC` · `O&M CAMC` · `cooling tower` · `AC plant operation`

## Eligibility glossary (always give the plain meaning)
- **EMD** — security deposit sent with the bid; refunded if you lose, adjusted if you win.
- **Turnover** — last year's total business; needs bank/ITR proof.
- **Experience certificate** — old client's written "we got this done by them".
- **OEM authorisation** — brand's paper that you are their authorised partner.
- **Class registration** — pre-obtained grading needed for big government works.
- **DSC** — digital signature token; required for most portal registrations.
- **CAMC** — maintenance with parts included; **non-comprehensive** = service only.
- **L1** — lowest bidder; **AOC** — award of contract; **PBG** — performance guarantee;
  **corrigendum** — official change to a tender (always re-check before bidding).

## Bid-size ladder (state this to a beginner explicitly)
1. Repeat-client AMC — no EMD, no registration, immediate cash.
2. Private-site jobs — direct pitch, negotiate freely.
3. **₹5–50 lakh** AMC/repair tenders (mostly GeM service bids) — first government target;
   small EMD (e.g. ₹46k on a supply+install job).
4. ₹1 Cr+ works — needs experience certificate + OEM paper + turnover + EMD in lakhs
   (reference point: a ₹1.35 Cr Hyderabad HVAC O&M CAMC on GeM; a Chiller-plant 3×150 TR
   AMC; UCIL AC/chiller AMC; CSIR cleanroom job at ₹26.3 Cr with ₹52.6 lakh EMD —
   that last one is *not* a beginner target).

## Capturing "ongoing contracts" (the real pipeline)
Government publishes **result / AOC pages** showing who won which contract for which
period. AMCs run 1–3 years, so the re-tender appears **2–3 months before expiry** —
that is the lead. Keep filling: Buyer / Work / Value / Last date / Contact / Status.
Cadence that works: 5 new rows per day in ~15 minutes.

## Cautions
- Snippet dates are not deadlines — **verify the last date on the portal** every time.
- Free aggregator listings lag behind the portal; the portal is the source of truth.
- Never bid without the experience certificate / OEM paper if the tender asks for it —
  the bid is rejected and the EMD is locked up.
