# India HVAC/R — competitor landscape (research 2026-09; re-verify before quoting)

Assign a year to every figure. These came from company sites, annual-report extracts,
market listings and a 2026 consumer survey; Indian private-company numbers move fast.

## Tier 1 — OEM brands with own service arms
- **Blue Star** — 32 offices, **1,060 service providers, 8,000+ technicians, 2 lakh+
  customers, ~25 lakh TR under service**, Tier 1–5 coverage. Moat = trained-people supply.
- **Voltas** (Tata) — biggest tier-2/3 reach, **5,000+ service centres**; own-brand only.
- **Daikin / Carrier / Johnson Controls-Hitachi / Trane / Mitsubishi Electric** —
  premium VRV/chiller tech, factory parts, warranty; expensive, slower, single-brand.
- **Lesson:** service capacity (trained technicians) is the moat, not the equipment.
  Become an **authorised service partner** to get brand credibility on bids.

## Tier 2 — large MEP/EPC contractors
- **Sterling & Wilson (SWREL)** — order book **₹2,243 Cr (Sep 2025)**, net worth
  **₹862.7 Cr**, but **FY25 EBITDA loss ₹123.95 Cr**, rating **BB-/A4**.
- Others: ETA Engineering, Zeco Aircon and similar MEP firms.
- **Lesson:** big order books ≠ profit. The stable money in this space is the
  aftermarket/O&M platform, not lump-sum installation.

## Tier 3 — consumer/aggregator platforms
- **Urban Company** — FY25 revenue **₹881 Cr (India services ₹694.8 Cr)**, **~40,000
  professionals, 12M+ customers, 59 cities**, listed Sep 2025; also operating in
  **KSA/UAE/Singapore** (JV route). Strong on residential split-AC servicing; publicly
  criticised for **part-price overcharging**.
- Aggregator complaints to study (2026 survey, 8,000+ responses): **67% say cost too
  high, 47% slow response, 42% hard to reach**; dissatisfaction with brand service
  **rose from 17% to 35% in three years**; brand AMC usage fell **13% → 7%**, while
  **44% prefer a local provider** (up from 38% in 2023).
- Home-services market ≈ **₹40,000 Cr**, growing **18–22%/yr**, **~80% unorganised**;
  platform AC technicians earn **₹52k–78k/month** (up from ₹40k–58k) — wage inflation
  is real, budget for technician retention.
- **Lesson:** trust + transparency + speed is the gap the brands left open. The
  residential side is contested; **commercial/industrial AMC is where the aggregators
  are weak.**

## Tier 4 — the actual competition (local + unorganised)
- Directory data: **5,126 HVAC contractors in India, 96.92% single-owner operations**
  (Apr 2026 listing data) — only a small minority belong to larger brands.
- Hyderabad/South India rivals to watch: **Arial Engineering Services** (~19 yrs,
  Madhura Nagar), Airtechno HVAC System Pvt Ltd, National Aircon India (NAIPL),
  Celsius HVAC Systems, SouthStar Engineering Solutions, Sriven MEP Engineering,
  Etna Air Conditioning, plus hundreds of unorganised technicians.
- **Lesson:** the fight is not against Blue Star. It is against a one-man shop that is
  cheaper but has no paperwork, no warranty, no BMS/validation capability. Win on
  written SLA, genuine parts, photo reports, GST bill, response time.

## Niche specialists worth benchmarking (pharma cleanroom)
**Fabtech Technologies** (IPO Jan 2026), **GMP Technical Solutions**, **iCLEAN**,
**Klenzaids** — turnkey cleanroom design/build. Cleanroom is investable and premium;
HMW's Dr Reddy's / Syngene / Aurigene experience is the entry ticket, paired with
validation documentation as a service.

## What HMW should copy (the actionable list)
1. Named AMC packages with written SLAs (OEM-style: visits/yr, hours-to-response, parts).
2. Build and retain a trained technician bench — capacity is the moat.
3. Register for OEM authorised-partner status and FM/PMC vendor panels (sub-contract flow).
4. Score the premium niches (cleanroom + data-centre cooling) instead of cheapest volume.
5. Fixed price + WhatsApp booking + visible reviews (the aggregator playbook, applied to
   commercial clients).
6. Publish client references/experience certificates and use them in every bid.
7. Avoid low-margin lump-sum EPC chasing; keep recurring AMC + spares as the core.
8. India-bench + KSA presence is a genuine differentiator (aggregators already operate
   in the Gulf; Indian clients' GCC operations are reachable).
