# The AI-default look — full refuse list and cures

If the first draft contains any of these, it is a template, not a design. Cut or invert.

## Visual cliches

- cream/beige ground + terracotta accent + acid-green badge (the current model-default palette)
- purple→pink gradient hero; glassmorphism cards over an abstract blob background
- centered hero, three-card feature grid, "Trusted by" logo strip, floating pill badges
- emoji as icons; `rounded-2xl` cards with a drop shadow on everything
- giant centered headline over a stock photo with a dark overlay
- every section the same height and rhythm (no pacing, no asymmetry, no silence)

## Copy cliches

- "Empower your business", "Seamlessly", "Unlock", "Elevate", "In today's fast-paced world"
- three-adjective headline stacks ("Fast. Simple. Powerful.")
- a CTA that says "Submit" / "Get Started" with nothing behind it

## Cures that read as human work

- one asymmetric proof block instead of three cards
- a single verifiable metric instead of a logo strip ("312 rooftops commissioned since 2021")
- a real diagram of what the client actually does instead of a lifestyle photo
- whitespace, one deliberate break of the grid, one accented word instead of an accented paragraph
- a named person's job title and a phone number that a human answers

## Imagery substitution

Client-facing work carries **no human faces and no living beings** (HARD RULE; exceptions: Aamir's
own photo on his own approved profile, and the fire-lab line). Replace people with:

- the product, the machine, the part, the surface, the material texture
- the site itself: racking, panels, pipework, cabling, a plant layout diagram
- light and environment: sky, sand, hard sun, night installation, inanimate detail shots
- abstraction: geometry, an isometric system diagram, a data visualisation of the client's numbers

Say the substitution out loud in the plan ("no people; subject is the machine, panel, surface") so
nobody re-adds a stock photo later.
