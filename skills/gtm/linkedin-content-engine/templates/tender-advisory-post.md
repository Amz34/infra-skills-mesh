131 words, hook in the first two lines, one idea, one CTA, mixed-case hashtags, no emoji, no phone.

Fill the `<>` slots. Keep it under ~140 words. The four figures are the whole post — do not
soften them into adjectives, and do not publish the client's own floor price or rate build-up.

## Generation rules

- Open with a first-hand observation ("Four days on one tender…"), not a claim about yourself.
- One bid only. Ten tenders dilutes the proof.
- Figures = what the bidder cannot see in the document (arrangement cash, at-risk cash, profit over
  term, extension upside). Percentages in "roughly", never a promise.
- Say the limit out loud in the CTA ("whether it is winnable") — that is what makes it read genuine.
- No phone in the body. No emoji. Hashtags 3-4, mixed case.
- Private-matter check BEFORE shipping: if the campaign that produced the figures is still live and
  exclusivity was promised to leads, offer a de-identified variant
  (`One recent <industry> <type> on <portal>. Rs <value>, <duration>.`) so the sender's own bid read
  does not become public knowledge for rival bidders.
- Offer to post (`/rest/posts` flow in SKILL.md); do not post unasked.
