## Competitive landscape — KSA exec search

- **Global giants** (Korn Ferry, Spencer Stuart, Heidrick, Egon Zehnder, Russell Reynolds + Riviera-type tech firms): 12-16 wk searches, USD 100-200K fees, weak technical fluency for engineering leadership.
- **Local boutiques** (e.g. Ruya, Expertise Recruitment, Hanson, InterSearch affiliates): faster but CV-shuffler positioning; thin process transparency.
- **White space confirmed**: no firm specializing ONLY in engineering/technical leadership for KSA built environment. That niche claim is defensible.

### Client-getting playbook benchmarks (from research)

- Riviera Partners: engineering-focused tech search; content moat = insights blog as sales tool.
- Hunt Club: 20,000+ expert referral network + social-graph mapping; 60-70% response vs 20-40% cold; pipeline <1 day; referral fee economics.
- Mercor: AI-native talent matching, $0 CAC / 100% referral growth; $350M raised @ $10B valuation (2025); clients incl. OpenAI/Anthropic/Scale.
- MSH: claims 48-hour shortlists.
- KiTalent (KSA): Proof-First model — market map + shortlist before fee; claims 7-10 working days to shortlist, 96% 1-year retention; interview-fee model option.

### Standard funnel (competitor baseline)

Intake → market map 50-80 names → discreet outreach → 20-40 discovery calls → 8-12 prelims → 5-8 shortlist dossiers → 3-4 finalists → 1-2 offers. Fees 25-35% first-year comp, min ~USD 80K, 10-14 weeks. Contrast with our locked SLA (kickoff 3d / map 2wk / shortlist 4-5wk / close 8-10wk).

## Regulatory — KSA (3 licensing layers)

1. **MISA Investor License** (foreign owners) + CR + Chamber of Commerce → office lease.
2. **MHRSD Recruitment License** — Local vs **International** variant. International recommended: covers hiring Saudi AND foreign pools. Processing ~2-3 months (some sources: up to 6). Without it, placement activity is illegal.
3. **Qiwa / GOSI / Muqeem / ZATCA VAT** — operational registrations.

- Unlicensed placement penalties: fines up to SAR 100K, suspension, permanent blacklisting.
- Labor Law: Saudi Labor Law M/51 (2005).
- Nitaqat Saudization quotas now extend to senior/technical roles → dual-track search (Saudi nationals + expats) is a compliance requirement, not optional — sell as Compliance-as-a-Product (most licensed firms execute it poorly).
- Entry strategy: start as advisory + talent-intelligence on a plain consulting CR (legal without placement license), run MHRSD application in parallel, upgrade when retained mandates confirm. Total incorporation ~6 months — start Day 1.
- KSA is not a one-stop license: recruitment, manpower supply, and headhunting may fall under different permits — verify current MHRSD portal classifications before committing.

## Regulatory — expat/freelance reality check (added 2026-09-11)
- **Freelance Work Document (Wtheeqa) = Saudi nationals only** (official condition: Saudi nationality + active Absher). Expats cannot obtain it. Its permitted specialist activity list *does* include "attracting job talent" — evidence the regulator separates talent attraction from licensed recruitment, but the document itself is closed to non-Saudis.
- Iqama ties work to the named sponsor + registered profession (MHRSD Art. 33/38). "Working for own account": fine up to **SAR 50,000 + deportation + work ban**; unlicensed business: fine + up to 6 months imprisonment + deportation. MOI re-warned Aug-2026 (Tagalog/Bengali/Indonesian); transaction monitoring live → Saudi-bank inflows for non-sponsored work are traceable.
- Unlicensed recruitment/outsourcing activity: reported **SAR 200,000–250,000**; MHRSD bulk-revoking/suspending recruitment office licences through 2026 (21 revoked + 4 suspended; 11 revoked in Q1-2026).
- Legal expat routes: **Premium Residency** (own business, any employer, no sponsor), **Ajeer** (temporary extra work, needs sponsor approval), employment/contract inside a licensed entity, or **MISA investor licence + CR**.
- **Pricing decides the licence class** → per-hire/success fee/%-of-salary = recruitment activity (licence). Flat fee per deliverable (market map, shortlist research, comp benchmark, assessment panel) = professional services (no recruitment licence). Structure and substance must match — never label a placement fee as "research".
- Compliant high-leverage play: sell the *technical assessment + market-map layer* to licensed Gulf recruiters (generalists, weak at AI/engineering vetting) on flat fees, run 3–5 partners, build the pool under their licences, then convert to own licence when Premium Residency/MISA lands.
